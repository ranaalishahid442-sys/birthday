✨ My Prayer For Your 20th Year ✨

"O Allah, bless her as she completes 20 beautiful years of life. Wrap her in Your endless light, protect her gentle soul, and grant her boundless health, radiant joy, and peace in her heart.

May every year ahead be sweeter than the last, and may our hearts always beat together in love and tranquility. Ameen."
